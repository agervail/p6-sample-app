Open this page from localhost

Over file://, Chrome gives the page an opaque origin: JavaScript modules and disk access are both blocked. From the project folder, run:

python3 -m http.server 8080

then open http://localhost:8080

    Importing into the P-6
    1. Connect your computer to the P-6’s USB port with a USB cable.
    2. Hold down the [SAMPLING] button while turning on the power.
    3. Once copying is finished, eject the “P-6” drive from your computer.
    4. Press the [KYBD] button.
